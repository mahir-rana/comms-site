The problem

When an event is under way, your team is scattered across a venue. Group chats bury the one message that matters, and nobody knows who is on duty, who is late, or whether the person at the entrance got the message. Comms replaces the group chat with one purpose-built line.

At a glance

📡 Live line

Tap in, see everyone on duty, and get alerts only while you're on.

🎛️ Soundboard

One-press alerts for the things that come up every event.

🚨 Urgent takeover

Full-screen alert with siren and haptics until someone responds.

🗓️ Shifts

Rota, clock in and out, handoffs, swaps and open shifts.

🚪 Entry check-in

Entry list, one-tap check-in, walk-ups and a live headcount.

📍 Location aware

Optional venue geofence tells you who has stepped away.

🛑 Shut down

A deliberate, can't-misfire way to stop an event on every phone.

📊 Recap

A shareable summary of the event, with light-hearted awards.

🔒 Private by design

Only your group sees your people, lists and activity.

How it works

Step 1

Sign in and join your group

  • Sign in with an email link, no password
  • Add your name and a profile photo
  • Join with an invite code or create your own group
  • Belong to several groups and switch in one tap
Step 2

Tap in

  • Live headcount with avatars of everyone on the line
  • Your next shift shown right in the lobby
  • Stay tapped in with the app closed until you tap out
  • You only get alerts while tapped in
Step 3

The soundboard

  • Send to everyone, to a title, or to whoever is on a post
  • Urgent keys are hold-to-send, so a stray tap can't fire one
  • Quick "On it" and "Seen" replies instead of a thread
  • Your team edits the board: add, rename, recolour, reorder
Step 4

Urgent takeover

  • The whole screen is taken over until someone taps ON IT
  • The sender sees a live count of who is responding
  • Arrives as a Time Sensitive notification when the phone is locked
Step 5

Shifts and accountability

  • Schedule grid of posts against time slots
  • Automatic reminders, clock in and out, one-tap nudges
  • Handoffs, swaps, drops and open shifts
  • Lock screen Live Activity for your next shift
Step 6

Entry check-in

  • Import an entry list from a CSV or paste in names
  • Loose name matching, so a typo still finds the right person
  • Walk-ups added on the spot, with a running headcount
  • Cap the entrance at capacity, with every change logged
Step 7

Recap

  • Totals: people in, team size, alerts sent, average first reply
  • How each shift went and who left the venue
  • Light-hearted awards, shareable as text

Built for trust and reliability

✅ Consent first

Alerts only reach people who are tapped in, and respect each person's own Focus settings.

🔒 Private by design

Email addresses are never visible to other members. Group data is readable only by that group.

🧱 Enforced on the server

Sensitive actions run server-side so a modified app can't fake them.

🩺 Built to be diagnosed

Push delivery is logged, and the app warns you if a phone isn't set up for alerts.
